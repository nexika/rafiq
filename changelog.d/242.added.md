On Windows, `rafiq ask`, `listen`, `dictate`, `panel`, `gestures` and `mcp` reach the running Rafiq through a named pipe only your account can open, and `rafiq panel` opens the panel as it does on Linux.
On Windows, dictation types what you say into the app you're in, Arabic and accents included whatever the keyboard layout, and hand gestures and the `media` tool work through the media keys and Ctrl+Tab.
On Windows, song recognition and "explain this video" hear what the computer plays, through the loopback of the default output (the `soundcard` package, now in the `windows` extra). `rafiq doctor` shows it as "The computer's own sound".
On Windows, Rafiq can look at the screen and click on it: "what's on my screen", a click on what you name after you say yes, and taking control of the computer for a task, with Escape or the Stop button to end it. Clicks land where they were circled on scaled screens and with several monitors.
On Windows, Rafiq says what is playing and in which app ("what's playing?"), and play and pause do exactly that, through Windows' media sessions (`winrt`, now in the `windows` extra): an open palm no longer starts paused music again.
On Windows, presence works: Rafiq notices your phone leaving and coming back to the home Wi-Fi and plays the away and welcome-home scenes. Before, a phone set in `[presence]` was never seen there, so the away scene could play while you were home.
