On Windows, `rafiq ask`, `listen`, `dictate`, `panel`, `gestures` and `mcp` reach the running Rafiq through a named pipe only your account can open, and `rafiq panel` opens the panel as it does on Linux.
On Windows, dictation types what you say into the app you're in, Arabic and accents included whatever the keyboard layout, and hand gestures and the `media` tool work through the media keys and Ctrl+Tab. Windows has one key for play and pause, so both toggle, and Rafiq can't say what is playing there yet.
On Windows, song recognition and "explain this video" hear what the computer plays, through the loopback of the default output (the `soundcard` package, now in the `windows` extra). `rafiq doctor` shows it as "The computer's own sound".
