Rafiq runs on Windows 10 and 11: install.ps1, the voice loop, settings in %APPDATA%\rafiq closed to other accounts with an ACL, the volume (the `windows` extra), notifications, opening apps from the Start Menu and pages, the clipboard in UTF-8, locking and power, and starting at logon as a Task Scheduler task without a window. `rafiq run` in a terminal opens the panel itself; `rafiq doctor` checks the Windows parts and lists what isn't on Windows yet (dictation, gestures, clicking on the screen, media keys, switching outputs, the computer's own sound, presence, the phone page, and `rafiq listen`, `dictate`, `panel` and `mcp`), which say so instead of failing.
